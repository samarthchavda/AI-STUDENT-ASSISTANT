## 🚨 Risk Mitigation

### Technical Risks
- **Server downtime:** Use Vercel/Render with 99.9% uptime
- **Database issues:** Daily backups on Supabase
- **API limits:** Monitor usage, upgrade plans proactively

### Business Risks
- **Competition:** Focus on differentiation (AI, pricing, all-in-one)
- **User acquisition cost:** Optimize ad spend, focus on organic growth
- **Churn:** Improve product, add more value, better onboarding

### Legal Risks
- **Content copyright:** Use original questions, cite sources
- **Data privacy:** GDPR compliance, clear privacy policy
- **Payment disputes:** Clear refund policy, responsive support
