"Company Sheets" in Admin Panel:

This section manages interview questions asked by specific companies (like TCS, Amazon, Microsoft, Google, Infosys, etc.).

What questions are stored:

Company-specific interview questions (both coding and technical)
Question text - The actual interview question
Category - DSA (Data Structures & Algorithms), System Design, etc.
Difficulty - Easy, Medium, Hard
Frequency - How many times the question was asked (helps rank popular questions)
Year asked - When the question was asked (e.g., "2024", "2023-24")
Topic - Specific topic like "Binary Search", "Dynamic Programming", "Arrays"
Solution outline - Brief hints/solution approach
Similar questions - Related problem references
Purpose:

Company-specific preparation - Students can practice questions that specific companies actually asked in interviews
AI Copilot integration - When students ask the AI about company interviews, it searches this database to provide relevant questions
SEO content - These questions help with search engine optimization (students searching "Amazon interview questions" etc.)
Bulk upload - Admin can upload CSV files with company question sheets
Example use case:

Student asks AI: "What questions does Amazon ask?"
AI searches this database and shows actual Amazon interview questions with difficulty, frequency, and topics
It's NOT for:

Aptitude/reasoning questions (those are in "Aptitude Bank")
Mock exam questions (those are separate)
Company Sheets = Real interview questions asked by companies (coding/technical focus)
